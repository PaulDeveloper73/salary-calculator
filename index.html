Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Important details about the calculations and your records.

How is gross salary calculated?

Gross salary is calculated by adding the basic salary and total allowances entered in the form.

How are deductions calculated?

The calculator applies the deduction percentage you enter to the gross salary. The percentage is user-defined and is not automatically derived from tax or social security rules.

Does this calculate Uganda PAYE and NSSF automatically?

No. This version uses a flat deduction-rate estimate. It does not determine statutory PAYE, NSSF, local service tax or other legally required payroll deductions.

Where is my calculation history saved?

The interface is designed for browser-local history. The actual storage behavior depends on the JavaScript implementation connected to this page. Clear history controls should be connected to that storage logic.

Can I use this for weekly or annual pay?

The pay-period selector helps label the salary figures you enter. Make sure the basic salary and allowances you provide use the same selected period. This interface does not automatically convert between pay periods.

Can I print or share my result?

The page includes controls for copying and printing a breakdown. Connect the controls to your JavaScript implementation to enable those actions.

Important payroll and tax notice

SalaryCalc Pro provides an indicative calculation based on the values and flat deduction rate entered by the user. It is not a statutory payroll, tax, accounting or legal compliance service. Actual take-home pay may depend on current legislation, tax bands, employee circumstances, benefits, pension contributions and employer-specific deductions. Verify payroll obligations with the relevant authorities or a qualified payroll professional before making payment or filing decisions.
